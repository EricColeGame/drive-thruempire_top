## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the best drive-thru empire strategy for beginners?
The most reliable beginner strategy focuses on order completion speed rather than kitchen variety. Upgrade your order intercom and main burger grill to cut order processing times under two seconds. Redeem starter cash codes early, avoid spending money on visual shop decorations, and pool your funds to unlock the second drive-thru lane as fast as possible.

### How do customer vehicles affect my restaurant profits?
Customer vehicles act as flat earnings multipliers on every completed meal ticket. While a standard starter sedan only pays baseline menu value (1.0x), rare hypercars and legendary space cruisers apply multipliers ranging from 8.0x to 100x per order. Upgrading your car pool with luck boosts is critical for late-game wealth.

### When should I use my luck boost codes?
Save your 2x luck boost codes until you have accumulated a substantial cash reserve ($100,000 or more) dedicated exclusively to rolling customer vehicles. Activating a luck boost without sufficient funds to purchase consecutive vehicle rolls wastes the temporary drop multiplier.

### Is the Moon expansion worth the high entry cost?
Yes. The Moon expansion unlocks anti-gravity multi-lane setups and celestial-class customer vehicles that pay hundreds of times more than Earth-based orders. Although the initial barrier to entry requires millions in capital, the return on investment quickly eclipses all previous restaurant tiers.
