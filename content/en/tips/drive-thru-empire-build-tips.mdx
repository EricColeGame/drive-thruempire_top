## Core Mechanics: Understanding Your Restaurant Flow

Before spending your hard-earned cash, you need to understand how the game's economic loop functions. The core loop consists of three interconnected systems: line management, kitchen preparation speed, and vehicle tier value. If any one of these pillars lags behind, your income hits an immediate bottleneck.

```
Incoming Customer ➔ Drive-Thru Window ➔ Order Processing ➔ Kitchen Fulfillment ➔ Cash Payout
```

When a car enters your driveway, its tier determines the baseline reward. However, money only transfers to your balance once the order completes. If your kitchen cooking speed is slow, expensive vehicles back up in the queue, stalling your turnover rate. Balancing your investments keeps vehicles clearing the pickup window without delay.

### The Profit Bottleneck Formula

Community reports show that profit drops off heavily when queue wait times exceed vehicle arrival intervals. Keeping your kitchen fulfillment rate slightly faster than your driveway influx ensures you never leave money sitting at the order intercom.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best drive-thru empire build tips for absolute beginners?
For new players, the best approach is to invest all early earnings directly into order window speed and base prep counters. Avoid spending money on cosmetic restaurant decorations or vehicle rolls until your kitchen reliably clears orders in under five seconds.

### How do I get more cosmic and exotic cars to show up?
High-rarity vehicles rely on luck multipliers. Save your vehicle rolls until you have an active luck booster running—such as a 30-minute promotional luck boost—and stack it with community weekend events whenever possible to maximize roll odds.

### Why is my drive-thru line backing up even after upgrading prep stations?
If cars are stacking up despite fast cooking times, your order window speed or lane capacity is likely throttling the operation. Upgrade the collection window and ensure your driveway is long enough to handle peak vehicle arrival waves without capping out.

### Should I prioritize automated workers early on?
Yes. Automation eliminates human clicking latency and keeps orders cycling smoothly. Transition to automated cooks as soon as the upgrade cost represents roughly 20–30% of your total bankroll.
