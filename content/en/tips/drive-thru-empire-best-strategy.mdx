Even dedicated players often stall their progress by falling into common traps that slow down progression:

- **Hoarding Cash:** Unlike games with high-interest bank mechanics, idle money earns nothing here. Reinvest your profits immediately to keep your multiplier compounding.
- **Ignoring Code Drops:** Developers frequently drop limited-time codes during update drops and weekend milestones. Skipping these means leaving free cash and crucial luck boosts on the table.
- **Over-Investing in Aesthetics Early:** Neon trim, custom signs, and parking lot landscaping look great, but they do not increase customer turnover during the early game.
- **Wasting Timed Boosts While AFK:** Activating a luck or cash boost and then walking away ruins your return on investment. Only pop boosts when you are actively managing customer flow.
