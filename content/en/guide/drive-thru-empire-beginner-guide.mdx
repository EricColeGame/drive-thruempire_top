## Core Gameplay Loop: How Drive-Thru Empire Works

At its heart, *Drive-Thru Empire* merges traditional tycoon automation with gacha-style car collection mechanics. You operate an order window where incoming vehicles line up for meals. Each vehicle that pulls up belongs to a specific rarity tier, dictating the base payout and order complexity. 

The gameplay loop consists of three interconnected phases:
1. **Preparing & Serving Orders:** Interacting with your kitchen appliances to cook burgers, fry sides, and pour drinks to fulfill customer orders before timers expire.
2. **Reinvesting Profits:** Spending earnings on kitchen automation, drive-thru lane capacity, and employee speed upgrades.
3. **Rolling for Rare Vehicles:** Utilizing the game's gacha roll system to unlock higher-tier customer vehicles that pull into your lanes and drop massive cash multipliers.

Understanding this flow allows you to balance kitchen capacity with drive-thru traffic. If you attract exotic cars before your cooking speed can handle them, order queues stall, dampening your hourly earnings.
