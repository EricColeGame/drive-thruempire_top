## Core Gameplay Mechanics: How Drive-Thru Empire Works

At its heart, the game revolves around two interdependent loops: servicing customer queues and rolling for higher-tier vehicles. When a patron pulls into your lane, your staff prepares the order and fulfills it at the service window. The transaction value depends primarily on the rarity of the visitor's car.

```
[Vehicle Roll / Spawn] ➔ [Drive-Thru Window] ➔ [Order Processing] ➔ [Cash Multiplier Payout] ➔ [Reinvest in Upgrades & Rolls]
```

Early on, basic sedans yield modest coin amounts. However, pulling high-rarity variants—such as Exotic or Cosmic rides—drastically scales your income per transaction. Balancing your budget between physical restaurant improvements and car rolls is the golden rule of efficient progression.
