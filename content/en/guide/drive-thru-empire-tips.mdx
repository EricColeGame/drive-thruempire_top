## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the most important drive-thru empire tips for absolute beginners?
Focus on station delivery speed first rather than rolling cars immediately. A fast kitchen clears low-tier orders rapidly, creating the reliable base capital required to fund high-percentage vehicle roll attempts.

### How do I get more cosmic and exotic cars to spawn?
Stack active luck boosts before purchasing vehicle rolls. Rolling during an active timed luck buff significantly increases your odds of securing rare car categories, which in turn drastically increases order payouts.

### Does upgrading restaurant decor improve my ticket values?
Cosmetic items and decor offer minor passive perks or aesthetic value, but they yield a much lower return on investment compared to grill speed, register throughput, and vehicle quality upgrades. Save cosmetics for the endgame.

### Why is my drive-thru line moving slowly despite high grill stats?
If food prep is fast but cars remain stuck, your window delivery speed is likely acting as a bottleneck. Upgrade the handoff counter and order speaker to ensure finished food is delivered immediately to waiting vehicles.
