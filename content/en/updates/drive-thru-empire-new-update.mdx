## Floor Plan Layouts for Maximum Throughput

A major pitfall players experience after applying the **drive-thru empire new update** is station bottlenecking. If your fryer sits all the way across the restaurant from your pickup window, your AI workers waste critical seconds running back and forth.

Optimizing your layout according to work triangle principles ensures your operation stays smooth even during midday rushes.

```
[Drive-Thru Lane Window]
          |
    [Assembly Table]
     /            \
[Drink Station]  [Grill & Fryer]
     \            /
   [Stockroom / Pantry]
```

### Optimal Upgrade Sequence for Beginners

To maximize income growth under the newly balanced economic system, follow this step-by-step upgrade blueprint:

1. **Expand Lane Capacity First**: Increase your driveway to at least 6 car slots so you never lose prospective walk-ins (or drive-ins) during station downtimes.
2. **Upgrade Assembly Table Speed**: The speed of placing items into bags is often the silent bottleneck slowing down pickup windows.
3. **Automate Drink Lines**: Beverage machines are fast to produce; assign your first automated NPC worker here to generate easy passive income.
4. **Invest in Radiant Grills**: Maximize burger output before touching the gourmet dessert machines.
5. **Unlock the Second Service Window**: This effectively doubles your peak capacity during special weather events or rush hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important change in the drive-thru empire new update?
The most impactful change is the addition of dual-service windows combined with tiered vehicle patience mechanics. This prevents high-paying sports cars from leaving prematurely while allowing players to service multiple vehicles at the same time.

### How do I unlock the new gourmet menu items?
You must reach Restaurant Level 18 and purchase the Turbo Radiant Grill or the Artisan Pizza Oven. Once placed inside your kitchen, gourmet recipes automatically appear in your order registry when high-tier cars enter your lane.

### Why are cars leaving my drive-thru lane without ordering?
Following the **drive-thru empire new update**, customers have dynamic patience timers based on their vehicle type. If your driveway is too short or your workers take too long assembling multi-item bags, cars will honk and leave. Upgrade your assembly table and extend your driveway capacity to fix this issue.

### Is the prestige reset worth doing after the patch?
Yes. Prestiged restaurants now gain an amplified bonus multiplier on all gourmet recipe tips, making recovery back to high levels much faster than in earlier versions of the game.
