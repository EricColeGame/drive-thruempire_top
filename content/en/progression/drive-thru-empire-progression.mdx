## Understanding the Core Game Loop and Currencies

Before diving into complex upgrade paths, you need to understand how the foundational engine works. Unlike pure idle games where passive income simply accumulates out of thin air, your revenue relies directly on your restaurant's physical layout and active vehicle flow.

```
[Roll Vehicles] ➔ [Place in Garage Slots] ➔ [Serve in Lanes] ➔ [Collect Cash & Gems] ➔ [Rebirth & Expand]
```

The game operates on a dynamic loop centered on three distinct phases:

1. **Build:** You expand your restaurant's physical operational capacity. This includes laying down additional drive-thru lanes, boosting vehicle movement speeds, decreasing car spawn delays, and unlocking extra vehicle slots.
2. **Roll:** You spend resources to draw randomized customer cars. Each vehicle comes stamped with a rarity grade and an inherent dollars-per-minute ($/min) generation value. High-value cars must be placed into garage slots to generate cash, while lower-tier duplicates can be discarded to free up room.
3. **Earn:** Placed vehicles actively pay out continuous passive income while your drive-thru processes them. Early runs generate modest amounts like $5 to $10 per minute, but veteran community reports highlight advanced builds skyrocketing past $700+ billion milestones.

## Early-Game Setup: The First $15,000 Milestone

The opening phase of the game can feel slow if you invest cash into the wrong upgrades. Many beginners fall into the trap of spending all their early money on car rolls before they have the physical infrastructure to process vehicles. 

In your first 30 minutes, your ultimate milestone is reaching approximately $15,000 in liquid capital to trigger your initial Rebirth.

### Optimal Upgrade Priority for Beginners

Always prioritize throughput over cosmetics or unslotted rolls. Follow this sequential order to maximize early cash flow:

1. **Unlock Lane 2:** Doubling your processing capacity immediately removes traffic bottlenecks.
2. **Spawn Speed (Level 1–3):** Reduces idle downtime between arriving customer cars.
3. **Car Movement Speed:** Ensures customers clear your window quickly after collecting their order.
4. **Garage Slots:** Every extra slot allows another income-producing vehicle to work simultaneously.

| Upgrade Type | Early Priority | Ideal Early Target | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lanes** | Highest | 2–3 Lanes | Directly multiplies the number of orders fulfilled at once. |

Community reports suggest avoiding heavy car rolling until you have at least 3 active garage slots open. A high-rarity car sitting in your inventory produces zero revenue if you lack the slot capacity to deploy it onto your lot.

## Rebirth Mechanics and Mid-Game Momentum

Once you cross the initial $15,000 threshold, you face your first Rebirth decision. Like most incremental games, rebirthing resets your active cash balance and basic lane levels in exchange for permanent global income multipliers and access to advanced systems.

Sticking to an un-rebirthed account causes your earning curve to flatten out. Embracing rebirths early accelerates your drive-thru empire progression exponentially over longer play sessions.

### Rebirth Checklist & Best Practices

Before hitting the rebirth button, run through these essential checks:

* **Max Out Active Slots:** Ensure your garage is packed with the highest $/min cars available so your post-reset recovery takes mere minutes.
* **Spend Floating Cash:** Cash is wiped during a rebirth, so spend leftover funds on any permanent upgrades or roll pools that persist.
* **Collect Spin Wheel Rewards:** Extra spins or saved bonus cash can instantly kickstart your post-rebirth economy.

| Rebirth Tier | Approx. Cash Requirement | Key Unlocks & Benefits | Recommended Strategy |
