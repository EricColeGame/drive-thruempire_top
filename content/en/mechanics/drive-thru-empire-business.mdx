## Core Mechanics of Your Drive-Thru Empire Business

At its heart, the game operates on a queue-and-fulfillment model. Vehicles enter the lot, place orders across multiple categories, wait for preparation, collect their food at pickup windows, and exit, generating income for your operation.

```
[Highway Entry] ➔ [Order Intercom] ➔ [Kitchen Fulfillment] ➔ [Service Window] ➔ [Highway Exit]
```

To prevent gridlock, every stage must process customers at comparable speeds. If your order stations take orders faster than your line cooks can prepare food, vehicles back up to the entrance, preventing new paying customers from entering the loop.

### Primary Operational Stations

Each station in your facility plays an interdependent role in maintaining overall revenue velocity:

| Station Name | Core Function | Primary Upgrade Metric | Key Bottleneck Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Order Intercom** | Takes initial customer orders | Order input speed | High queue times back up into the highway |
| **Prep Counters** | Assembles base food items | Preparation time / capacity | Kitchen starvation blocks pickup window |
| **Beverage Dispenser** | Fills drink side items | Pour speed / auto-dispense | Drink delays pause combo pickups |
| **Pickup Window** | Delivers final meal to vehicle | Hand-off duration | Long dwell times stall queue progression |
| **Parking & Drive Lane** | Holds awaiting traffic | Max vehicle buffer slots | Overflow prevents new cars from entering |

Balancing these nodes requires systematic reinvestment. Prioritizing only the pickup window will starve your cash register if your prep counters cannot fulfill food orders rapidly enough.

## Strategic Upgrade Prioritization

Knowing where to put your gold ensures rapid expansion into new areas and prevents costly progression freezes. Pouring excessive capital into vanity decorations or secondary counters early on often slows progress. 

A disciplined approach to running a **drive-thru empire business** involves cycling upgrades through distinct operational phases.

| Phase | Upgrade Target | Target Ratio | Strategic Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Foundation** | Order Intercoms & 1st Prep Station | 1:1 Level Ratio | Establish baseline customer intake and quick initial gold |
| **Phase 2: Flow Balancing** | Pickup Windows & Secondary Items | 2:1 Window vs Counter | Prevent cars from idling at the collection point |
| **Phase 3: Capacity Scaling** | Lane Extensions & Counter Slots | Max Lane Buffer | Absorb peak traffic rushes without losing incoming cars |
| **Phase 4: Automation** | Staff Automation & Card Levels | Max Available Upgrades | Transition from active tapping to passive idle generation |

### Key Milestones for Station Upgrades

* **Level 10 Station Buffs**: Typically unlock a 1.5x speed modifier.
* **Level 25 Automation**: Enables basic AI staffing to remove manual input requirements.
* **Level 50 Product Tier**: Introduces higher-value menu items that instantly boost gross income per transaction.
* **Level 100 Mastery**: Halves base production cycles and increases card slot effectiveness.
