## How to Find and Safely Join the Official Community

To safeguard your account, always navigate through verified portals:

1. **Roblox Experience Page:** Check the social links section directly beneath the description on the official [Roblox platform](https://www.roblox.com). Legitimate developers anchor their Discord invites there.
2. **Developer Social Handles:** Check the verified Twitter/X or YouTube channels linked by the game's development group.
3. **In-Game Settings Menu:** Many tycoon games embed a direct "Socials" button inside the main menu.

## Community Rules, Safety, and Scam Prevention

To maintain a secure trading and chat environment, moderators strictly enforce standard rules. Violating these can result in an instant mute or permanent ban from the **drive-thru empire discord server**.

### Essential Rules to Remember

- **No Cross-Trading:** Trading in-game assets, restaurants, or currency for off-platform items, real money, or Robux violates both server rules and the Roblox Terms of Service.
- **Respect Staff and Members:** Constructive criticism about game updates is welcomed, but harassment directed at developers or volunteers leads to swift administrative action.
- **Keep Media in Context:** Reserve vehicle photos, gameplay clips, and fan art for their designated showcase channels.

If an unknown user direct messages you claiming to be a moderator offering a "Developer Menu" or "Free Infinite Cash Glitch," block them immediately. Legitimate staff members will never ask for your login credentials or request files to be downloaded outside of official platforms.

### Where can I find working promo codes in the drive-thru empire discord server?
Check the `#codes` or `#announcements` channels. Whenever the game updates or reaches an engagement milestone, developers post codes that grant cash bonuses, speed boosts, or limited restaurant decorations.

### How do I participate in community parking lot car meets?
Head over to the `#car-meets-and-events` channel within the server. Hosts post VIP server links, rules, and theme requirements. Ensure your vehicle matches the event criteria and turn down graphics settings if the lobby has many participants.

### What should I do if my restaurant progress resets?
If a server desync or bug wipes your progress, do not panic. Open a ticket in the `#support` or `#bug-reports` channel of the **drive-thru empire discord server**. Provide your Roblox username, approximate time of the incident, and any screenshots or video clips you have of your previous build.

### Are VIP servers required to host large community events?
While not strictly required, VIP servers are highly recommended. Public servers contain random players who may intentionally ram cars or block your drive-thru lanes, disrupting organized meets and lowering customer turnover rates.
